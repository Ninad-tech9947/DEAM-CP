Digital electronics project · Pune

Pune Power Brain

Every glowing node is a real part of Pune's grid. A model predicts each one's demand 30 minutes ahead, every second, and a digital circuit switches off places before an overload. Hospitals, water, fire, police, telecom and transport always stay on.
